# CAMPUS UNIVERSITY - CENTRAL LIBRARY & STUDENT CLUBS GUIDE

## 1. Central Library Hours and Borrowing Privileges
- **Operating Hours**: Monday to Friday: 08:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Weekends and Exam Weeks: Open 24 Hours.
- **Digital Library**: Access to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect, and SpringerLink available on campus LAN and remote VPN.
- **Book Circulation**:
  - Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books for 14 days.
  - Postgraduate & Research scholars may borrow up to 8 books for 30 days.
  - Overdue fine is $0.50 per day per book.
- Discussion rooms and silent reading cabins are reservable through the library kiosk.

## 2. Student Clubs and Societies
- **Technical Clubs**: ACM Student Chapter, Robotics & Automation Club, Coding Ninjas, Google Developer Student Clubs (GDSC), Aerospace & Drone Society.
- **Cultural & Arts Clubs**: Music Society (Symphony), Dramatics Club (Abhinaya), Photography & Fine Arts, Dance Crew (StepUp).
- **Social & Leadership**: Rotaract Youth, Literary & Debating Society, National Service Scheme (NSS), Entrepreneurship Cell (E-Cell).

## 3. Sports & Health Facilities
- Olympic-size swimming pool, 4 synthetic badminton courts, 2 basketball courts, cricket ground with floodlights, and a gymnasium with personal trainers.
- University Health Centre: 24/7 emergency care, resident medical officer, free generic medicines, and ambulance service.
