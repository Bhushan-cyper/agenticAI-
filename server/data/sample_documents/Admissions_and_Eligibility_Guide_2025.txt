# CAMPUS UNIVERSITY - ADMISSIONS & ELIGIBILITY GUIDE 2025-2026

## 1. Undergraduate Engineering Admissions (B.Tech)
- **Eligibility Criteria**: Candidates must have secured a minimum of 75% aggregate marks in 10+2 (or equivalent) examination with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Computer Science.
- **Accepted Entrance Exams**: Admissions are offered through JEE Main (All India Quota - 60% seats) and State CET (State Quota - 40% seats).
- **Application Deadline**: Online registration for Round 1 opens on May 15, 2025, and closes on June 30, 2025. Late submissions are accepted until July 10 with a late fee of $50.
- **Seat Allocation**: Total intake for B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering is 180 seats, Data Science & AI is 60 seats, Electronics & Communication is 120 seats, and Mechanical Engineering is 60 seats.

## 2. Postgraduate Admissions (M.Tech & MBA)
- **M.Tech Requirements**: Valid GATE score in respective discipline with minimum 60% in undergraduate engineering degree.
- **MBA Requirements**: Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 50% aggregate and valid score in CAT / MAT / CMAT exams. Group discussion and personal interview are conducted in July.

## 3. Required Documents for Verification
1. Class 10th and 12th original mark sheets and passing certificates.
2. Entrance Exam Score Card and Admit Card.
3. Transfer Certificate (TC) and Migration Certificate.
4. Category/Caste Certificate (if claiming reservation).
5. Four recent passport-sized color photographs and Government ID (Passport/Aadhaar/National ID).
6. Medical fitness certificate signed by a registered medical practitioner.

## 4. International Student Admissions
International candidates can apply under the NRI/Foreign Quota. Required documents include valid passport, student visa eligibility, and SAT scores (minimum 1200) or equivalent high school credential evaluation.
