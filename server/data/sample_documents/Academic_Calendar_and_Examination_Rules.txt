# CAMPUS UNIVERSITY - ACADEMIC CALENDAR & EXAMINATION RULES

## 1. Autumn Semester Key Dates
- **Orientation & Registration**: August 1 - August 4.
- **Commencement of Classes**: August 5.
- **Mid-Semester Examinations**: October 6 to October 14.
- **Techno-Cultural Fest (InnovateX)**: November 12 to November 14.
- **Last Teaching Day**: November 28.
- **End-Semester Final Exams**: December 5 to December 20.
- **Winter Vacation**: December 22 to January 5.

## 2. Spring Semester Key Dates
- **Class Commencement**: January 6.
- **Mid-Semester Examinations**: March 3 to March 10.
- **Annual Sports Meet (Olympus)**: March 24 to March 26.
- **End-Semester Final Exams**: May 4 to May 18.
- **Summer Vacation & Internships**: May 20 to July 31.

## 3. Attendance and Grading Regulations
- **Minimum Attendance**: 75% attendance in theory and laboratory classes is strictly required to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
- **Medical Leave Condonation**: Up to 10% relaxation is permitted on genuine medical grounds certified by the university health center doctor.
- **Grading Scale**: Absolute 10-point scale:
  - 90-100% -> Grade 'O' (Outstanding, 10.0)
  - 80-89% -> Grade 'A+' (Excellent, 9.0)
  - 70-79% -> Grade 'A' (Very Good, 8.0)
  - 60-69% -> Grade 'B+' (Good, 7.0)
  - 50-59% -> Grade 'B' (Above Average, 6.0)
  - Below 40% -> Grade 'F' (Fail, 0.0)
