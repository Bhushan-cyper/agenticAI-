# CAMPUS UNIVERSITY - HOSTEL, MESS & RESIDENTIAL RULES

## 1. Accommodation & Amenities
- Accommodation is provided in 4 Boys Hostels (Aryabhata, Ramanujan, Kalam, Bose) and 3 Girls Hostels (Gargi, Kalpana, Sarojini).
- Room types available: Double occupancy (AC and Non-AC) and Triple occupancy.
- Amenities include high-speed Wi-Fi, 24/7 power backup, study lounges, laundry facilities with automated token systems, and indoor sports equipment.
- Annual Hostel Fee: Non-AC Double Sharing ($1,800/yr), AC Double Sharing ($2,400/yr).

## 2. Mess Timings and Dining Policy
- **Breakfast**: 07:30 AM to 09:30 AM (Nutritious menu including cereals, eggs, south/north Indian cuisines, fresh fruits, tea/coffee).
- **Lunch**: 12:15 PM to 02:15 PM (Multi-course balanced meal with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options twice a week).
- **Evening Snacks & Tea**: 05:00 PM to 06:15 PM.
- **Dinner**: 07:45 PM to 09:45 PM.
- Special dietary requests or medical meal preparations can be coordinated through the Chief Mess Warden.

## 3. Curfew, Leave & Security Regulations
- **Campus Entry Curfew**: All hostel residents must return inside campus gates by 10:00 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends.
- **Night Out / Outstation Leave**: Requires digital pass approval from the Hostel Warden through the student portal, approved at least 24 hours in advance with parent/guardian consent.
- **Zero Tolerance Policies**: Ragging, smoking, alcohol consumption, unauthorized appliances (heaters/hotplates), or harboring non-residents in rooms results in strict disciplinary action and possible expulsion.
