# CAMPUS UNIVERSITY - FEE STRUCTURE & SCHOLARSHIPS POLICY

## 1. Annual Tuition & Academic Fees
- **B.Tech (CSE / AI / ECE)**: $8,500 per academic year ($4,250 per semester).
- **B.Tech (Mechanical / Civil)**: $7,000 per academic year.
- **M.Tech Programmes**: $6,000 per academic year.
- **MBA Programme**: $9,200 per academic year.
- **Security Deposit (Refundable)**: $500 one-time fee payable at the time of admission.
- **Examination Fee**: $150 per semester, due two weeks before mid-term examinations.

## 2. Payment Schedules and Due Dates
- **Autumn Semester**: Fee payment window is July 1 to July 25. Late payment incurs a penalty of $10 per day up to August 10.
- **Spring Semester**: Fee payment window is December 15 to January 10.
- Payment modes accepted: Online Net Banking, Credit/Debit card portal, Wire transfer, or Demand Draft.

## 3. Merit and Need-Based Scholarships
1. **President's Merit Scholarship**: 100% tuition fee waiver for top 5 rankers in entrance examinations with aggregate > 95% in high school.
2. **Dean's Excellence Scholarship**: 50% tuition waiver for students maintaining a CGPA >= 9.0 in subsequent semesters.
3. **Sports & Cultural Achievement Waiver**: 25% to 50% tuition concession for national-level sports champions and recognized performing artists.
4. **Need-Based Financial Aid**: Up to 75% fee relief for students from economically disadvantaged families (annual family income < $15,000). Applications must be submitted to the Student Aid Committee with income tax returns.
