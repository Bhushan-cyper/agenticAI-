# CAMPUS UNIVERSITY - PLACEMENT & INTERNSHIP BROCHURE 2024-2025

## 1. Placement Statistics & Highlights
- **Placement Rate**: 94.6% of registered eligible students placed in the 2024 graduating batch.
- **Highest International Package**: $145,000 / annum (Offered by Global Tech Corp, USA).
- **Highest Domestic Package**: $48,000 (INR 42 LPA).
- **Average Package (Overall)**: $12,500 (INR 10.8 LPA).
- **Average Package (CSE & AI)**: $16,800 (INR 14.2 LPA).
- **Total Companies Visited**: 240+ recruiting partners including Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Cisco, Goldman Sachs, Deloitte, TCS Research, and Adobe.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria for Placement Drives
- Minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 6.5 with no active backlogs for standard recruitment drives (Dream/Super Dream companies may mandate CGPA >= 8.0).
- Mandatory attendance in Training & Placement Cell (T&P) soft skills, coding assessments, and mock interviews.
- "One Student One Job" policy applies; students holding an offer above $15,000 qualify for Dream Upgrades.

## 3. Summer Internship Policy
- Mandatory 8-week summer internship at the end of the 6th semester for B.Tech students.
- Average monthly internship stipend: $800. Highest monthly internship stipend: $2,500.
- Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs): 38% of interns secured full-time employment conversion prior to 7th semester.
